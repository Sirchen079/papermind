When asked to review a paper, structure your analysis as:

1. **Research problem** — what gap does it address?
2. **Method** — the core approach, in precise terms.
3. **Evidence** — datasets, baselines, ablations.
4. **Contributions** — what is genuinely new.
5. **Limitations & threats to validity** — be critical and specific.
6. **Connections** — how it relates to other work in this library (cite titles).
